### What the design team must deliver

1. **Research & IA:** one approved sitemap for Home, Projects, Project Detail, Services, About, Contact; 3 primary stakeholder flows (industrial investor, civil project owner, contact).
2. **Brand-aligned digital system:** color tokens, heading/body type styles, 12/8/4-column grids, spacing, media crop/contrast rules, iconography and baseline accessible contrast.
3. **Figma file:** at least 12 core artboards (6 templates × desktop/mobile), tablet responsive rules, at least 25 semantic components/variants and full interactive states. Motion prototype is a separate working reference.
4. **Engineering handoff:** navigation/menu cases, filters including 0 result, image error, no-approved-media behavior, contact error/success/fail-closed, bilingual text overflow and keyboard/reduced-motion behaviors.
5. **Content needs:** approved crop list and rights, each project’s contractual role and technical evidence. No photo production is included unless separately contracted.
6. **Design QA:** 6 viewport classes, 2 design review cycles (initial direction + detailed UI), targeted task testing with 5 B2B stakeholder proxies before design freeze.

### Proposed roles (part-time, overlapping)

- Creative/UI Lead: oversees concept, photography curation, brand system and design acceptance.
- UX Designer: information architecture, project discovery, inquiry workflow, mobile patterns.
- Interaction Designer: transition timings, prototype, reduced motion and clickable behavior.
- Design QA/Handoff owner: Figma annotations, state matrix, build parity review.

One designer can perform multiple roles; hours are a planning allocation, not compulsory payroll levels.

### Excluded from the $5,000

Original photoshoots, professional video production, brand/logo redesign, bilingual translation service, legal content, logo licensing, hosting, cloud subscriptions, CMS production implementation, marketing campaigns, third-party component/template licenses, and developer engineering hours.
